## Working with Patrick

Working style: the main session is the steer and must stay free to reply to Patrick instantly. Any task that takes more than a quick check or a one-line answer runs in a background subagent, without being asked. Run independent pieces in parallel. Reply to every message right away with status, and write every reply so it reads well aloud: short, plain sentences, no tables or long lists unless asked (Patrick listens with the Claude app's own read-aloud button). Do not send MP3 clips; Patrick found the player's button unusable. Never use em dashes in text for Patrick.
- Species names: enter every name exactly as Patrick says it or as the card prints it. Never replace it with modern nomenclature or a synonym, and never invent authors or Italian names he did not give. Only fix obvious transcription spelling.
- Transcription: use 29 s Whisper windows plus a second pass offset by 14 s, never 15 s windows (they lose words at the joins).
- Times: always speak in Patrick's local time (Sicily, Europe/Rome: CEST until 25 Oct 2026, then CET). Never say UTC.
- Publishing the app: bump APP_VERSION in the page before each publish and then write db doc config/versione {v, quando} with the same number, so the web app shows the update banner.
- Re-filming protocol (new species arrive and specimens move): before any write, export a full backup of specie and foto to the scratchpad and commit it. Match every name in a new video against the catalogue by exact name and by synonym. Already catalogued: update its zone and spot (and add the new photo), never a second entry. Not catalogued: add it. Unsure match, unreadable name or conflict between voice and card: do not write, ask Patrick. Never delete a species because it was not seen in a new video; list it as "not seen today" for Patrick to decide. After each batch, read back a short diff: added, moved, unchanged, not seen, to check.
- Replanting (from 23 Oct 2026): today's photos show specimens on plates with handwritten notes. Only after Patrick's explicit go-ahead: back up foto, then clear all foto records (keep the backup and the species), and attach new photos from the new videos of the replanted beds with the printed plastic cards. Read each printed card: it confirms or corrects names, authors and the "da confermare" doubts; report every change of name before or as it is applied. Positions follow the new videos under the re-filming protocol.
- Edibility on the printed cards (from 24 Oct 2026): the filled circle at the bottom of each card gives `commestibilita` on the species. Green = commestibile, yellow = non commestibile, blue = commestibile solo dopo prolungata cottura, red = tossico, skull = mortale. Read it from the card; if the colour is unclear, ask Patrick rather than guess.
- Never guess what Patrick has already shown or told us. Before building or asking, brief every agent to use the existing evidence first: config/layout, config/sala, the room-walk videos (142241, 142710), the backups and the earlier findings in the scratchpad. Ask Patrick only what that evidence truly cannot answer.
- Verify before stating a cause, a speed, a count or a status. Check the logs, the db and the store first, and give the numbers. If it cannot be checked yet, say "I do not know yet" and say how you will check. Never offer a guess as an explanation.
